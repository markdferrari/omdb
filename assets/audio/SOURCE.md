# Provisional audio previews

`preview_music.wav` and `preview_sfx.wav` were generated in this repository with Python's
standard `wave`, `math`, and `struct` modules on 2026-10-08. No external recordings or
third-party samples are used. Mono PCM16 at 22,050 Hz: a two-second eight-note sine
melody with exponential envelopes and a 0.16-second descending cartoon tone.

These sources make independent Music/SFX volume and mute controls testable. They are
not final US7 music or hazard-specific sound design; audibility and tone at actual
speakers/headphones remain unverified in the headless environment.
